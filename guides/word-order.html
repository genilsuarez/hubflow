Word Order Untangled

English word order is far stricter than Spanish — here's the skeleton every sentence hangs on.

🏗 Basic Word Order (SVOMPT)

Subject + Verb + Object + Manner + Place + Time
Unlike Spanish, English rarely moves the subject or object around for style — the order is fixed.
She (S) drank (V) coffee (O) quickly (M) at the café (P) this morning (T).

⌛ Adverb Placement

Frequency adverbs go mid-sentence
Always, often, never, usually go between the subject and the main verb — but after "to be".
She always drinks coffee. She is always late.
💡 Never at the start = inversion
Putting a negative adverb (never, rarely, hardly) at the front of a sentence for emphasis forces question-word-order in the main clause.
Never have I seen such a mess.

❓ Question Word Order

Auxiliary + Subject + Verb
Questions invert the subject and the auxiliary verb. Wh-questions add the question word first.
Do you exercise often? How often do you exercise?
