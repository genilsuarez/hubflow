🤝 Hedging & Softening

Academic and professional English states claims cautiously. Overstating reads as naive, not confident.
Modal verbs
This may indicate…
The results could suggest…
Reporting verbs
The data suggest / indicate / imply…
Quantity hedges
Most participants… A tendency towards…
Distance
It appears that… It has been argued that…
Softened requests
I was wondering whether you could…
Softened disagreement
I'm not entirely sure that…
The findings suggest that stress may affect recall.
The findings prove that stress always affects recall.

🎯 Precision Over Grandeur

Formality is not a synonym for length. A precise short word beats a vague long one, and a Latinate word used loosely is a worse error than a plain word used well.

VaguePrecise alternatives
importantcrucial · decisive · influential · substantial
big changesharp rise · gradual shift · complete overhaul
showdemonstrate · reveal · illustrate · confirm
bad effectadverse impact · detrimental effect · side effect
talk aboutaddress · examine · outline · discuss
Register is chosen by the reader, not the writer. Before editing, name the audience and the channel — a lecturer, a client, a colleague on chat — and let that decide how far up or down to move.
