Dictation Strategy

What to do while the audio plays, what to fix afterwards, and the sounds that disappear in connected speech.

Strategy Sound traps Punctuation

🎧 While the Audio Plays

Three passes beat one careful pass.
Pass 1 — shape
Do not write. Catch the topic, the tense and how many clauses there are.
Pass 2 — content words
Write nouns, main verbs, numbers and names. Leave dashes for what you miss.
Pass 3 — function words
Fill the dashes with articles, prepositions and auxiliaries. Grammar predicts most of them.
After the audio
Read your sentence as a sentence. Repair agreement, endings and spelling from what must be true.
Function words are unstressed, not absent. If you hear a gap between two content words, something small is there — usually a, the, of, to, and or have reduced to /ə/ or /əv/.
