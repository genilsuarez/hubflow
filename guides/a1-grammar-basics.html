📌 To Be & Have

The two verbs that carry most A1 sentences — and the two that Spanish speakers mix up most.
SubjectbehaveExample
IamhaveI am tired. I have a car.
you / we / theyarehaveThey are late. We have time.
he / she / itishasShe is a nurse. He has two sisters.
I am 30 years old. / I am hungry.
I have 30 years old. / I have hungry.

English uses be for age, hunger, thirst, cold, heat and fear — where Spanish uses tener. Keep have for possession and for family, illnesses and appointments.

Negatives and questions: be needs no helper — She is not here. Is she here? But have takes do — She doesn't have a car. Does she have a car?

its vs it's: its = possessive (the dog wagged its tail). it's = it is / it has. The apostrophe never marks possession on a pronoun.

👈 This, That, These, Those

Two questions decide the word: near or far? one or many?
Near (here)Far (there)
Singularthisthat
Pluralthesethose
this / these
In your hand, in this room, happening now.
This coffee is cold.
These shoes are new.
that / those
Across the room, over there, already mentioned.
That building is old.
Those people are waiting.
On the phone: English says This is Ana for yourself and Is that Ana? for the other person.

✏️ Contractions

Spoken English contracts by default. Not contracting sounds emphatic or formal, never neutral.
FullContractedFullContracted
I amI'mis notisn't
you areyou'reare notaren't
he is / he hashe'sdo notdon't
we havewe'vedoes notdoesn't
they willthey'llcannotcan't
I would / I hadI'dwill notwon't
Never contract a short answer. Yes, I am. — not Yes, I'm. The same goes for Yes, she is and Yes, they are. Negative short answers do contract: No, I'm not.

☛ Imperatives & Instructions

Base verb, no subject. The shortest sentence English has.
Affirmative
Base form, nothing before it.
Open the window.
Turn left at the bank.
Negative
Don't + base form.
Don't touch that.
Don't be late.
Suggestion
Let's + base form (= let us).
Let's go.
Let's not argue.
Softened
Add please, or reframe as a question.
Please sit down.
Could you sit down?
Be careful. / Don't be careless.
You be careful. / Not be careless.
