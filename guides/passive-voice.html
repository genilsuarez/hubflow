Passive Voice Made Clear

One formula, four tenses — turning the object of an action into its subject, so the doer can stay hidden or unimportant.

🔨 The Basic Formula

1Present Simple
am / is / are + past participle
General facts and routines.
English is spoken all over the world. • Dinner is served at 8pm.
2Past Simple
was / were + past participle
A completed action in the past.
The bridge was built in 1932. • The letters were sent yesterday.
3Present Perfect
have / has been + past participle
A past action with a present result.
The documents have been signed already. • The house has been sold.
4Future
will be + past participle
A planned or predicted future action.
The report will be published next month.

🎯 When to Use It

👤 The doer is unknown or unimportant
When who did the action matters less than the action itself.
My car was stolen last night. This phone was made in China.
📋 Formal, scientific or news writing
Passive voice sounds more objective — common in reports, research, and headlines.
The results were analysed over six months. Three suspects were arrested today.
💡 Adding "by + agent"
Only add by + someone when the doer is genuinely important new information — otherwise, leave it out.
The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci. The window was broken. (nobody cares who — omit "by")
