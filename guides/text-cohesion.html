Text Cohesion

The small words that stop a text from repeating itself — and what exactly they point back to.

📌 Reference Words

it / this / these
It refers back to one thing already named. This can refer to a whole previous idea, not just one noun. These refers back to two or more things.
I bought a phone. It is very fast. The team lost. This surprised everyone.
The former / the latter
When two options were just mentioned, the former = the first one, the latter = the second one. Formal, mostly written English.
He was offered two jobs; the former paid more.

🔄 Substitution Words

so / one
So replaces a whole clause after verbs like think/hope/believe. One replaces a singular countable noun already mentioned.
Is he coming? I hope so. I need a phone — mine is an old one.
💡 either / neither
Either agrees with a negative statement. Neither means "not one nor the other" — referring back to two things.
I don't like coffee. My brother doesn't either. Neither of them called.
